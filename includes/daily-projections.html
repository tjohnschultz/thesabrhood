Three of four activation stages are now underway

The scheduled slate and player simulations feed a permanent pregame ledger. Version five adds recent starter workload, contact-quality context, a validated manager hook, named bullpen choices, and environment-aware plate appearances; the next work is proving that full construction on future games.
