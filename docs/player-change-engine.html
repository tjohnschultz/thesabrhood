MLB player change detection with recent-versus-prior z-scores and full-season league percentiles.

Player intelligence

What changed—and how good is it?

The Trend Engine sifts six hitter and pitcher measures, finds each player’s most unusual recent movement, and keeps the full-season league context beside it.

Two lenses, one readChange z-score shows how many league standard deviations a player’s recent movement sits from ordinary movement in that statistic. Season percentile compares the full-season level with league peers.
