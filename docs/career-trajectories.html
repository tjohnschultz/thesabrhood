Career Path Engine

Chronologically backtested MLB hitter career projections built from age- and game-aligned historical paths.

Career-Outlook Engine

Career Outlooks

The Career Path Engine finds historical hitters who reached the same number of games at a similar age with a similar offensive profile, then shows what happened next as a range. Its reliability is measured with roll-forward tests that never allow a future career into an earlier forecast.

Comparable paths, translated for readersThe engine aligns hitters by career games, age, offensive shape, and playing-time pace. The public view emphasizes what those careers suggest; calibration diagnostics remain in the methodology files.
