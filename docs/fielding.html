Fielding

Official Fielding Run Value, Gold Glove Watch, Play of the Day, and adjusted runner-advancement prevention.

The other half of run prevention

Turn every defensive play into a decision

Range converts the ball in play. The arm changes the runner’s choice. The catcher controls strikes, blocks, and throws. The Fielding Engine keeps those skills separate, then presents them as a game-day story, a season leaderboard, and a matchup tool.

Fielding Engine v1

Defense, down to every play

Official Statcast Fielding Run Value anchors the public board. SABRhood development models add credited batted-ball opportunities and runner-advancement prevention without presenting those estimates as official tracking metrics.
